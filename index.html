SurgeOS Gmail

A privately operated newsletter-processing and research-digest application.

This is not a public service. SurgeOS Gmail is operated privately by its owner for their own research. It is not offered to the public, and there is no way to sign up for it or connect another account.

What it does

SurgeOS Gmail works with a single Gmail account that its operator has explicitly authorized through Google's OAuth consent process. Using that authorization, it:

  • Reads newsletters — it searches the authorized account for messages from a small, fixed set of industry and venture-capital newsletter senders and reads their text.
  • Prepares research summaries — it processes that newsletter text to identify companies and developments relevant to the operator's private research.
  • Sends digest emails — it sends the resulting research digest by email from the authorized account to a recipient chosen by the operator (by default, the same account).

It does not modify, label, move, or delete any email. Apart from the newsletter searches described above, and confirming that its own digest emails were sent, it does not access other messages.
